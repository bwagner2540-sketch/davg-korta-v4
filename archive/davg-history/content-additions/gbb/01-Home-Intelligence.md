### Website copy

#### Choose how much of the home to bring together

A good control system makes the household easier to run. Begin with the daily actions that deserve one clear command.

##### A focused start

Bring selected rooms or routines into one coordinated experience.

- Start with a media room, key lighting scenes or another clear priority.
- Choose controls that the household can use immediately.
- Leave an expansion path where it is useful.

##### A connected residence

Coordinate supported lighting, shades, entertainment and climate across the home.

- Define room and whole-home scenes together.
- Select interfaces around the people who use each space.
- Plan the network, wiring and control system as one design.

##### A more extensive property

Extend the system across a larger or more complex residence and its related spaces.

- Account for detached buildings, exterior zones and equipment access.
- Size the control architecture around the actual system.
- Document how the home is operated and supported.

Controller selection follows the equipment, integrations and performance brief. It is not a public package label.

## Put in Section 07 — Interfaces and household use cases

Module ID: `household-interfaces`

**Placement:** Add before the household moment / exception table. Replace any remote → touchscreen → voice quality ranking; keep the use-case and manual-route table.

### Website copy

#### Choose the controls people will reach for

Remote, touchscreen, keypad, phone and voice can work together. The best mix depends on the room, the task and the person using it.

##### In hand

A remote provides familiar control for everyday viewing and listening.

- Consider Halo or Halo Touch for a compatible Control4 system.
- Keep common entertainment actions easy to find.

##### At the wall or table

A touchscreen or keypad gives a shared control point that does not depend on someone having a phone.

- Use a compatible Control4 touchscreen for broader system access.
- Use scene keypads for quick, repeatable actions.

##### By voice

Voice adds another way to call supported actions when speaking is convenient.

- Consider a compatible Josh.ai integration where it suits the household.
- Keep a physical control available for the same routine.

The goal is a familiar action in each place, rather than a different interface for every system.
