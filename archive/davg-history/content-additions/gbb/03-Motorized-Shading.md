### Website copy

#### Three shading paths

Start with the opening and the effect you want from the fabric. Then choose the treatment, mounting detail, power and control path.

##### Finished-home simplicity

A battery-powered roller approach can bring shading to selected rooms without running a new power cable to every opening.

- Consider Triathlon; compare Serena where its system and control options fit.
- Check fabric, opening size, battery access and integration.
- Use a mount that respects the finished room.

##### Integrated architectural shading

A Sivoia QS-based design can support a more coordinated shading plan, with the treatment and power path selected for the project.

- Coordinate rollers, drapery or other supported treatments.
- Plan pockets, blocking, wiring and service access.
- Choose compatible lighting and whole-home controls.

##### Exposed design detail

Palladiom roller shades make the bracket and finish part of the architecture.

- Choose visible hardware with intention.
- Select wired or wire-free equipment to suit the opening.
- Coordinate alignment, fabric and neighboring finishes.

A wireless control connection does not necessarily mean battery power. Confirm communication, power and platform compatibility as separate choices.
