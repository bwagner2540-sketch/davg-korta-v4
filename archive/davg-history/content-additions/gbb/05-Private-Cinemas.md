### Website copy

#### Choose the cinema around the seats

The cinema brief starts with the people in the room: where they sit, what they watch and how far the experience should reach.

##### A dedicated private cinema

Create a purposeful room with controlled light, an appropriate image and surround sound.

- Establish seat count and sightlines first.
- Select a display or projection system for the room.
- Address acoustics and simple operation.

##### An immersive projection room

Develop the screen, speaker layout and room treatment around a more demanding brief.

- Consider an acoustically transparent screen where it fits the design.
- Plan surround and height channels around actual geometry.
- Coordinate bass, ventilation and equipment access.

##### A reference-oriented cinema

Pursue a higher performance target through a more extensive room and system design.

- Specify image, sound and every-seat goals together.
- Evaluate processing and equipment appropriate to those goals.
- Include measured calibration and detailed construction coordination.

Channel count and equipment price cannot substitute for the room design. The proposal should make the performance goals and construction scope clear.
