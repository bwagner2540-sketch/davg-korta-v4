### Website copy

#### How lighting fits into whole-home control

Whole-home control and the lighting system have different jobs. Control4 can coordinate supported lighting with media, shades and other systems; the lighting architecture still needs to suit the loads and construction.

##### Local dimming

Compatible dimmers control loads near the room, with scenes coordinated through the selected system.

- Useful when adapting existing wallboxes.
- Confirm load and dimmer compatibility.

##### Scene keypads

A keypad gives the household a simple way to call several lighting actions together.

- Name buttons for familiar routines.
- Choose a control that belongs to the selected lighting platform.

##### Centralized or integrated lighting

A larger project may use panelized Control4 lighting or a compatible Lutron system integrated with Control4.

- Coordinate wiring and panel access early.
- Keep local lighting operation and household override clear.

These approaches can coexist. A keypad is an interface, not a substitute for the load-control design.

### Website copy

#### Three ways a HomeWorks brief can grow

HomeWorks scope is shaped by the lighting design and installation conditions, rather than a fixed processor count or a house-size label.

##### Selected architectural spaces

Concentrate coordinated control where the lighting detail matters most.

- Define the rooms, loads and scene requirements.
- Resolve control locations and access to equipment.

##### Whole-residence lighting

Bring the residence into one planned lighting and shading experience.

- Coordinate floors, exterior zones and daily scenes.
- Plan wiring, panels, controls and commissioning together.

##### Expanded tunable-light design

Add compatible tunable light, such as Ketra, where color and quality of light form part of the brief.

- Design the fixtures and control system as one package.
- Agree the scenes and schedules the household will actually use.

The proposal separates lighting equipment, fixtures, controls, electrical coordination and commissioning so the investment is understandable.
