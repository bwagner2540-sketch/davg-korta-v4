### Website copy

#### Choose how the property should gather

Outdoor entertainment begins with the places people actually use. Plan sound, viewing and control around those places and their exposure.

##### A patio experience

Create a focused listening or viewing area close to the home.

- Place suitable outdoor speakers near the listeners.
- Match any display to the actual sun and weather exposure.
- Keep controls within easy reach.

##### Connected outdoor spaces

Coordinate dining, lounge, pool or garden areas with distinct zones.

- Distribute sound for useful coverage at comfortable levels.
- Plan source grouping, bass and independent volume.
- Coordinate cable routes, power and outdoor network access.

##### A property-wide plan

Bring several outdoor destinations into a coordinated entertainment design.

- Develop landscape audio and viewing areas around the site.
- Plan conduit and equipment access with hardscape and planting.
- Set sensible boundaries for sound and light.

Weather exposure, trenching, hardscape and service access can shape the scope as much as the equipment.
