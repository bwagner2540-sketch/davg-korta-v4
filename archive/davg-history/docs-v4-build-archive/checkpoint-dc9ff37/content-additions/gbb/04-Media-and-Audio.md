## Put in Section 06 — Which speaker type, and why Sonance or Triad?

Module ID: `sonos-audio-paths`

**Placement:** Add after the existing Sonance / Triad speaker-type guidance. Keep Sonance / Triad, speaker count, TV choice and Atmos content. Sonos does not replace those decisions.

### Website copy

#### Choose how music enters the room

Streaming, amplification and speakers form different parts of the audio design. Sonos can support room-based listening or an architectural system; speaker placement still follows the room.

##### Room by room

Standalone Sonos speakers are a simple way to bring streaming music to selected spaces.

- Place speakers around the listening area.
- Group compatible rooms when shared music is useful.

##### Built into the room

Sonos Amp can drive compatible passive speakers, including Sonos Architectural by Sonance.

- Choose in-ceiling, in-wall or outdoor speakers for the intended location.
- Plan wiring, amplifier access and speaker coverage.

##### Coordinated across the home

A wider distributed-audio design brings sources, amplification, zones and control into one plan.

- Choose equipment around the room and zone requirements.
- Coordinate compatible audio with whole-home scenes when useful.

Architectural audio is about coverage and placement as much as concealment. Select the streaming and amplification layer alongside the speaker design.

#### Let the room start with one familiar action

A supported Control4 scene can coordinate the equipment involved in a routine, so starting a film or listening to music takes fewer separate steps.

##### Watch

Bring the selected display, source and sound system into the right state.

- Include lighting and shades where the selected integrations support it.
- Leave volume and source changes easy to reach.

##### Listen

Start a chosen source in the rooms that belong in the moment.

- Keep local volume and grouping understandable.
- Define which zones should stay independent.

##### Finish

Return the room to an agreed state when the activity ends.

- Turn off the intended equipment.
- Use a clear manual route when the household wants something different.

## Put in Section 12 — What changes the investment?

Module ID: `media-room-scopes`

**Placement:** Add after the existing media investment introduction. Replace any competing media-room package ladder; keep cost categories and the earlier TV / speaker / Atmos chapters.

### Website copy

#### Choose the room experience

A media room can remain a flexible living space or become a more focused place for watching and listening. The scope follows how the room will be used.

##### An elevated living room

Improve the display, sound and everyday operation of a shared space.

- Match the screen to light and viewing distance.
- Choose a soundbar or speaker layout that fits the room.
- Make daily controls familiar.

##### A room built around entertainment

Give picture and sound a more deliberate place in the architecture.

- Plan front speakers, surrounds and bass around the seats.
- Consider acoustic treatment and equipment access.
- Coordinate lighting, shades and control.

##### Performance with a quieter visual presence

Integrate the experience with custom joinery, concealment and finish details.

- Resolve concealed equipment and service access early.
- Coordinate screen, speaker and mounting details with the design team.
- Keep the performance brief clear beneath the finish work.

Concealment adds design and installation scope. It does not automatically make the room sound better.
