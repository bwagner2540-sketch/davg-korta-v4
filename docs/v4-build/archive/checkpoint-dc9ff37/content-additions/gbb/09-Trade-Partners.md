## Bring technology into the project at the right time

The useful starting point depends on whether the home is finished, under construction or still being designed.

### An existing home

Work from the rooms, equipment and pathways already in place.

- Identify the changes with the greatest everyday value.
- Plan retrofit access and protect the finished space.

### A build or renovation underway

Coordinate technology before walls and finishes make changes difficult.

- Align equipment locations, wiring and mounting requirements.
- Resolve responsibilities with the builder, electrician and relevant trades.

### An early design collaboration

Bring lighting, shading, media and infrastructure into the architectural conversation.

- Develop the technology brief alongside the room and property plans.
- Identify visible details, concealed space and service access early.

Start with the plans or the current site conditions. From there, define a clear scope and the next useful step.
