## Chapter 5 — website copy and system comparison

**Heading:** Now coordinate the room.

**Lead:** The roller shade and the control system do different jobs. The shade manages the window. The system coordinates shades, lighting and the way you operate the home. We choose both together so the hardware, controls and everyday behavior fit the project.

| Path | Homeowner explanation | Likely starting situation | What must be confirmed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Focused shade control | A compatible remote or supported Lutron hub/system can manage selected shades without making a whole-home lighting project necessary. | A defined room or shading-only improvement | Exact shade/remote/hub compatibility and whether grouping/app/schedules are required |
| RadioRA 3 | Coordinate compatible shades and lighting through a professionally configured wireless control system, with supported keypads, remotes, app operation and scenes. | Existing homes, remodels or new homes whose scope suits its supported devices and topology | Shade RF path, keypad power, coverage and current supported models |
| HomeWorks | Plan lighting and shading as a broader residential control architecture, including architectural keypad choices and the appropriate wired/wireless infrastructure. | Projects needing its system scope, control choices or construction coordination; often custom builds and substantial remodels | Processor, shade links, keypad wiring and system design; existing-home eligibility is project-specific |

**Bridge copy:** If you already have Lutron controls, we begin there. A shade addition may extend the current system. A larger project may justify a different architecture. The decision follows the home and the desired controls—not a rule that every retrofit uses RadioRA 3 or every custom build needs HomeWorks.

**Control4 companion:** Where the home uses Control4, compatible Lutron systems can participate in broader household scenes. Control4 is the integration layer in this explanation, not a fourth Lutron shade family. Confirm the installed processor and supported driver before promising functions.

Show a small diagram with separate physical and control paths: fabric/roller → compatible Lutron shade/control link → selected Lutron system → keypad/app; optional Control4 coordination branch. Do not imply the keypad directly powers the motor or that wired shade power means wired communications.

## Chapter 6 — actual keypad education

**Heading:** Choose the control you’ll actually use.

**Lead:** A shade can have its own open/close control, or join a room scene with the lights. The keypad should make those choices obvious and suit the wall where it lives. We resolve the button layout, labels and finish with the control system.

| Control | System/context to show | What the homeowner should see and understand |
|---|---|---|
| Pico remote | Compatible shade/system configurations; wall, tabletop or handheld as specified | Simple accessible control without assuming every wall position needs a mains-powered keypad. Show the actual selected shade/scene version, not an unrelated lighting-only face. |
| Sunnata RF keypad | RadioRA 3 version | 2-button; 3-button with raise/lower; 4-button variants. Show how fewer/more buttons change the daily task. RF communication does not make this keypad battery powered; its installation requires appropriate power. |
| seeTouch | Correct RadioRA 3-compatible model; relevant HomeWorks model separately if included | Clearly labeled button-based control. Photograph the selected exact model and engraving; do not use one seeTouch image to imply universal system compatibility. |
| Palladiom keypad | HomeWorks residential model | Flat architectural face and coordinated finishes; show real front, wall and close-up views. Distinguish Palladiom keypad from Palladiom roller hardware. One does not automatically require the other. |
| Alisse | HomeWorks QSX wired QS-link model | Architectural metal face, circular buttons and personalized engraving. Show chosen configuration and finish. It is not a RadioRA 3 keypad upgrade. |

The verified Sunnata variants are specific to the RadioRA 3 specification reviewed. Other platforms may have similarly named product versions; identify the exact platform/model in the manifest. Do not display commercial/myRoom product photographs as interchangeable residential controls.

**Button-story example:** A three-scene illustration might label Morning, Privacy and Evening, with raise/lower on a compatible configuration. Label it proposed programming. Use real hardware geometry and keep conceptual button labels in a live adjacent diagram if no matching photographed engraving exists. Never fake an official engraved product photograph.

## Chapter 8 — dedicated design/build coordination copy

**Heading:** Designed for the stage your home is in.

**Lead:** The same daylight goal can require very different work in a finished room and a home still on paper. We plan the shade around the construction opportunity, then coordinate the details with the people responsible for the window, ceiling and controls.

| Project | Homeowner copy | Architect / builder / designer coordination |
|---|---|---|
| Existing home / Retrofit | Start with the windows you want to improve and the controls already installed. We assess fit, support, fabric, access and available power before recommending battery or wired options. | Existing trim, mounting support, finish protection, access and any targeted electrical work |
| Remodel | An open ceiling or changed window is an opportunity to resolve a cleaner detail. Decide layers, mounting and power while the affected work is accessible. | Revised elevations, pocket sections, blocking, power routing, finish interfaces and service opening |
| Custom build | Bring shading into the window and lighting plan early. Coordinate each opening’s fabric, assembly and controls before framing and electrical decisions are fixed. | Opening schedule, glazing/elevations, pocket/roller sections, support, electrical responsibilities, keypad locations and finish/engraving schedule |

**For architects, builders and interior designers:** We coordinate the technology portion with the architectural and interior design intent: shade assemblies, power/control requirements, proposed keypad locations and the interfaces requiring agreement. Structural, architectural and electrical work stays assigned to the appropriate project team. Do not present DAVG as an architecture practice or imply licensed services not supplied.

**Recently purchased a home?** Identify the existing shades, processor, controls and access arrangements before replacing hardware. Explain what can be retained and what needs service or reconfiguration.

**Preparing a handoff?** Document the equipment, everyday controls, applicable account ownership/access and service contact. Coordinate transfer and remove former-owner access as appropriate for the actual system; do not promise automatic ownership transfer for every platform.
